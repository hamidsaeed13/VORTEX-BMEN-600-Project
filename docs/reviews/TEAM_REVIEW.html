Suggested replacement interpretation for PR 14:

In the exploratory unweighted cross-validation, CBC increased mean AUROC beyond age and sex. The observed gain persisted in a sensitivity analysis excluding participants below operational hemoglobin cutoffs; this does not establish that anemia or other red-cell effects are absent. The red-cell ablation must be rerun after clarifying its feature block. BMI/waist, hs-CRP and iron-marker additions produced small observed differences, whose paired uncertainty has not yet been estimated. These findings do not establish clinical utility or population-level performance.
